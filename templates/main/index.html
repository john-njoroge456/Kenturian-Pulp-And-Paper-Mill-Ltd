WHO WE ARE

About Us

Kenturian Pulp & Paper Mills Limited is a Kenyan paper manufacturing company committed to turning recovered paper resources into dependable, high-quality paper solutions. We combine responsible resource use, manufacturing expertise and a commitment to customer needs to create value for businesses and support a more sustainable paper industry.

Purpose

Our purpose is to turn waste into value — delivering quality paper while reducing environmental impact.

OUR COMPANY

Our Business

We manufacture a versatile range of paper products, including Kraft paper, Test Liner, Duplex Paper, Tissue and MG Cover Paper. Available in different colours, sizes and specifications, our products are developed to meet diverse packaging and industrial requirements while delivering consistent quality and reliable performance.
