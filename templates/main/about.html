OUR STORY

Turning Waste Into Value

Kenturian Pulp & Paper Mills Limited was built on a simple idea: that waste can be transformed into something valuable. What began as a small operation has grown into a trusted Kenyan paper manufacturer, serving businesses with quality paper products tailored to their needs. Today, we continue to build on that foundation by combining responsible resource use, manufacturing expertise and a commitment to our customers — creating value from recovered materials while contributing to jobs, enterprise and a more sustainable future.

OUR JOURNEY

Milestones

2018

Company Incorporated

Kenturian Pulp & Paper Mills Limited was incorporated, laying the foundation for a locally focused paper manufacturing business.

2019

Groundbreaking

Construction and development of the manufacturing facility commenced, marking the beginning of Kenturian’s journey towards local paper production.

2020

PM1 Assembly

Assembly and installation of the first paper machine, PM1, was undertaken, establishing the foundation for Kenturian’s manufacturing operations.

2021

Operations Begin

Kenturian commenced production with a single production line, initially focusing on tissue paper to meet the growing needs of the local market.

2023

PM2 Commissioned for Kraft Paper

The second paper machine, PM2, was introduced to expand production capabilities into Kraft paper, strengthening Kenturian’s product portfolio.

2024

PM1 Transitioned to MG Cover Paper

In response to changing market demand, PM1 was reconfigured from tissue production to MG Cover Paper, expanding Kenturian’s capabilities and enabling the business to better serve its customers.

Today

Expanding Our Capabilities

Kenturian continues to invest in cleaner production processes, strategic partnerships and new manufacturing capabilities, with PM3 planned to expand the portfolio into Duplex Paper production as part of the company’s next phase of growth.
